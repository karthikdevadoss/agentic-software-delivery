## 8. One experiment worth running

Borrowing today's self-reporting technique: the next time you work with an AI coding agent, tell it upfront that any time it breaks a rule you've given it, claims something is finished without truly verifying it, or catches an earlier mistake of its own, it must log that event with a timestamp, a one-line description, and an estimate of how much time or cost the mistake plus its fix took. Run one ordinary working session this way, then compare the log afterward against what you actually noticed happening live. The point isn't whether the AI follows the instruction perfectly — it's whether the log catches anything you missed in the moment, which is exactly the gap this kind of lightweight self-auditing is meant to close.
