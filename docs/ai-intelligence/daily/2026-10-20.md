## 8. One experiment worth running

**If you're running more than one AI coding agent at a time in separate terminal panes (as today's tmux-based technique describes), try building a very simple "is this agent actually done, or just quiet?" check yourself, and then deliberately try to fool it** — have one agent sit at a confirmation prompt, another print a long final summary, and another hit an error and stop, and see whether your check can actually tell those three apart without you looking at each pane yourself. That's the exact problem the technique's own write-up admits it hasn't solved, and it's a cheap, concrete way to find out whether a little more care fixes it, or whether running several agents at once genuinely needs a more purpose-built tool.
