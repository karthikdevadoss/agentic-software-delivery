Home page options

Four distinct concepts for the first page an HR screener, recruiter or hiring manager sees. Each is a working static mockup built on verified facts only.

Prepared 2026-09-29 · Research and reasoning in RESEARCH_AND_OPTIONS.md · Nothing here is deployed or linked from the running platform.

  1. Option A

    Thirty Seconds

    A scannable brief. Who he is, what he does, three pieces of proof, one click to a running system. Designed around the six-to-eight second first scan and the roughly one minute a screener gives a whole application.

    Best for: HR screener · lowest risk of misreading
  2. Option B

    The Proof Wall

    Every claim carries a verification level and a link to the running thing — and a short list of what is deliberately not built. Answers the 2026 problem directly: applications are assumed AI-polished until something is checkable.

    Best for: technical recruiter and hiring manager · strongest trust signal
  3. Option C

    One System, In Depth

    A single engineering case study told properly: the problem, the design decision, the failure drill, the measured result. Built for the reviewer who wants to see how he thinks rather than how much he has shipped.

    Best for: hiring manager and architect-level screening
  4. Option D

    What Are You Hiring For?

    Three doors — backend lead, agentic AI lead, platform architect — each mapping the same real evidence to a different role. Built for skills-based screening, where the reader is matching a requirements list rather than reading a biography.

    Best for: recruiter working from a requirements list

How to read these. Every claim on every mockup traces to a verified artifact in this repository or to a live production page; the trace table is in the report. Placeholders are marked TODO and listed as Owner decisions.

Not included on any mockup, on purpose: employment dates, phone number, address, salary, certifications, invented metrics, and any claim that portfolio work ran at employer production scale.
